Geography & Weather

About Japan & Hokkaido

🗾
Japan is an island country
日本是一个岛国 (Rìběn shì yīgè dǎoguó)
It's made of 4 big islands and thousands of tiny ones, sitting in the ocean next to China and Korea.
⬆️
Hokkaido is at the very top
北海道在最北边 (Běihǎidào zài zuì běibiān)
It's Japan's 2nd biggest island, like the "hat" on top of the country. It's the coolest, snowiest part!
🐄
Home of milk and cheese
牛奶和奶酪的故乡 (Niúnǎi hé nǎilào de gùxiāng)
Hokkaido has SO many dairy cows that most of Japan's milk, butter and cheese comes from here.
🌸
Famous for purple lavender fields
薰衣草花田 (Xūnyīcǎo huātián)
In summer, Furano's hills turn purple with lavender flowers! By September the flowers are mostly done, but the green hills are still pretty.
🐻
Wild bears live here
这里有野生的熊 (Zhèlǐ yǒu yěshēng de xióng)
Hokkaido has real wild brown bears in the forests — that's why we stay on paths and listen to the grown-ups!
🌡️
September weather: jacket time
九月的天气：要穿外套 (Jiǔyuè de tiānqì: yào chuān wàitào)
It won't be snowing yet, but mornings and evenings are cool — like the start of autumn. Bring a light jacket!
✏️
They write with kanji, just like Chinese!
用汉字写字 (Yòng hànzì xiězì)
Japanese writing uses kanji characters borrowed from Chinese long ago, plus two extra alphabets called hiragana and katakana.
Where You're Going

Places on Your Trip

🏔️ Tomamu (トマム)

占冠 · Zhàn guàn

A mountain resort where you're staying first! Wake up early and you might see the Unkai — a "Sea of Clouds" — where you stand ABOVE the clouds like you're floating!

mountain 山 shāncloud 云 yúnforest 森林 sēnlín

🧀 Furano (富良野)

富良野 · Fùliángyě

A farming town famous for cheese and lavender! You'll visit a real cheese factory and eat pizza and cheese soft-serve ice cream.

cheese 奶酪 nǎilàofarm 农场 nóngchǎngice cream 冰淇淋 bīngqílín

🎨 Biei (美瑛)

美瑛 · Měiyīng

Rolling hills that look like a patchwork quilt from above, plus the Blue Pond — water so bright blue it looks painted!

hill 山丘 shānqiūblue 蓝色 lánsèpond 池塘 chítáng

🍜 Asahikawa (旭川)

旭川 · Xùchuān

A city famous for ramen noodle soup! There's a whole "Ramen Village" with lots of shops, plus maybe a visit to a famous zoo.

noodles 面条 miàntiáocity 城市 chéngshìzoo 动物园 dòngwùyuán

⛵ Otaru (小樽)

小樽 · Xiǎozūn

An old seaside town with a pretty canal, glass-blowing shops, music boxes, and delicious sushi!

canal 运河 yùnhésushi 寿司 shòusīglass 玻璃 bōlí

🌋 Lake Toya (洞爺湖)

洞爺湖 · Dòngyé hú

A round lake sitting inside an old volcano! You'll ride a cable car up a smaller volcano nearby and might see fireworks over the lake at night.

lake 湖 húvolcano 火山 huǒshānfireworks 烟花 yānhuā
